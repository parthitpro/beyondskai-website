Cancellation & Refund Guidelines

We understand that travel plans can change. Below are our standard cancellation rules:

  • Flight tickets are governed by individual airline refund and cancellation terms.
  • Hotel cancellations depend on property deadlines (Free cancellation vs non-refundable rates).
  • Embassy visa fees paid to government portals are strictly non-refundable under all circumstances.
  • Approved refunds are processed back to the original payment source within 5-7 business days.
