Andaman Horizons: Thailand Phuket & Krabi Escape

The Journey Overview

Experience the dazzling turquoise waters and karst limestone cliffs of southern Thailand. Enjoy a private speedboat cruise to Maya Bay & Phi Phi Islands, discover the emerald lagoons of Krabi's 4 Islands, and unwind at beachside resorts.

Handcrafted by Beyond Skai with 4-star beachfront resort stays, English-speaking island guides, and private vehicle transfers.

Day-by-Day Experience

Day 1
Arrival in Krabi & Ao Nang Beach Sunset

Arrive at Krabi International Airport. Meet your private chauffeur for transfer to your Ao Nang resort. Spend the evening strolling along the beachfront promenade enjoying fresh coconut ice cream and beachside cocktails.

Day 2
Krabi 4-Islands Speedboat Excursion

Board a fast speedboat to explore Koh Poda, Tup Island (walk on the sandbar separating two islands at low tide), Phra Nang Cave Beach, and Chicken Island with crystal clear snorkeling.

Day 3
Scenic Transfer to Phuket & Big Buddha

Chauffeur drive from Krabi to Phuket across lush rubber plantations and dramatic limestone landscapes. Visit the iconic Big Buddha viewpoint overlooking Chalong Bay and check into your Patong/Kata beach resort.

Day 4
Phi Phi Islands, Maya Bay & Snorkeling

Full-day luxury speedboat tour to the legendary Phi Phi Leh, Maya Bay (filming site of The Beach), Pileh Lagoon for swimming in turquoise waters, Viking Cave, and Monkey Beach with Thai buffet lunch.

Day 5
Phuket Heritage Old Town & Departure

Explore Sino-Portuguese architecture and vibrant street art in Old Phuket Town before your private chauffeur transfer to Phuket International Airport (HKT).
