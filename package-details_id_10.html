The Journey Overview

Journey through the cradle of classical civilization and the sun-drenched islands of the Aegean Sea. Handcrafted by Beyond Skai, this 8-day itinerary seamlessly weaves ancient Athenian marvels, dramatic Santorini caldera sunset cruises, and the glamorous bohemian charm of Mykonos.

Enjoy premium handpicked cliffside suites, private chauffeur airport/port transfers, high-speed sea ferries, and skip-the-line guided explorations.

Day-by-Day Experience

Day 1
Arrival in Athens & Private Hotel Transfer

Land in historic Athens. Our representative warmly welcomes you for a private chauffeur transfer to your city center hotel. Enjoy a leisurely evening exploring the vibrant Plaka district under the illuminated Acropolis.

Day 2
Acropolis Marvels & Ancient Athenian Heritage

Embark on a private guided tour of the Acropolis, Parthenon, and the New Acropolis Museum. Spend the afternoon sampling authentic Greek cuisine and artisanal coffee.

Day 3
High-Speed Ferry to Mykonos & Little Venice Sunset

Board the premium fast ferry across the sparkling Aegean Sea to cosmopolitan Mykonos. Check into your boutique resort and watch the iconic sunset from Little Venice with panoramic windmill views.

Day 4
Mykonos Island Beaches & Delos Exploration

Relax on pristine turquoise beaches like Psarou or Super Paradise. Optional yacht excursion to the sacred UNESCO sanctuary island of Delos.

Day 5
Ferry to Magical Santorini & Oia Caldera Views

Cruise into the volcanic caldera of Santorini. Check into your cliffside retreat in Oia / Fira. Unwind as the white-washed buildings glow golden at dusk.

Day 6
Luxury Sunset Catamaran Cruise, Hot Springs & BBQ

Sail across the volcanic islands, swim in natural thermal hot springs, snorkel at Red Beach, and savor a fresh Greek BBQ meal accompanied by local Santorini wines as the sun sets over the caldera.

Day 7
Santorini Winery Tour & Coastal Charm

Visit award-winning cliffside wineries, explore the traditional village of Pyrgos, and enjoy a farewell dinner overlooking the deep blue sea.

Day 8
Departure Transfer & Fond Farewell

Enjoy your final Greek breakfast before your private transfer to Santorini Airport (or ferry back to Athens) for your journey home with memories that last forever.
