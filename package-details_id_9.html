Day-by-Day Experience

Day 1
Arrival in Dubai & Marina Dhow / Yacht Cruise

Arrive at Dubai International Airport (DXB). Meet your private chauffeur for transfer to your luxury hotel in Dubai Marina. In the evening, embark on a private yacht cruise gliding past the illuminated skyscrapers and Ain Dubai.

Day 2
Dubai Frame, Museum of the Future & Burj Khalifa

Marvel at the iconic Dubai Frame bridging old and new Dubai, explore futuristic exhibits at Museum of the Future, and watch the Dubai Mall Fountain Show before ascending the Burj Khalifa observation decks.

Day 3
Old Dubai Gold Souk & Premium Desert Safari

Morning stroll through the historic Al Fahidi district and Spice & Gold Souks with an abra boat ride across Dubai Creek. In the afternoon, head into the golden dunes for 4x4 dune bashing, camel riding, and a gourmet 5-star Arabic BBQ dinner under starlit skies.

Day 4
Palm Jumeirah, Atlantis Aquaventure & The View at The Palm

Visit the man-made marvel Palm Jumeirah. Enjoy 360-degree vistas at The View at The Palm and spend an adrenaline-filled day at Atlantis Aquaventure Waterpark and Lost Chambers Aquarium.

Day 5
Abu Dhabi Grand Mosque & Louvre Day Trip (Optional)

Take a private day excursion to the capital Abu Dhabi to visit the breathtaking Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, Emirates Palace, and Louvre Abu Dhabi museum.

Day 6
Luxury Shopping & Departure Transfer

Leisure morning for shopping at Mall of the Emirates or Dubai Mall. Private chauffeur transfer to Dubai Airport for your flight back home.
