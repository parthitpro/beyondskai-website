The Journey Overview

Surrender to the tropical allure of Bali. Wake up to floating breakfasts in your private pool villa in Seminyak, take in emerald rice terrace views in Ubud, and cruise by speedboat to the dramatic turquoise cliffs of Nusa Penida (Kelingking Beach & Broken Beach).

Handcrafted by Beyond Skai with dedicated English-speaking private chauffeurs, romantic candlelight dining, and Balinese spa rituals.

Day-by-Day Experience

Day 1
Arrival in Denpasar & Seminyak Beach Sunset

Welcome flower garland at Ngurah Rai International Airport. Private chauffeur transfer to your Seminyak beachside resort. Spend a relaxed afternoon exploring beach clubs or enjoying a traditional sunset at Double Six Beach.

Day 2
Nusa Penida Island Speedboat Day Excursion

Board the fast speedboat from Sanur to Nusa Penida. Explore the breathtaking T-Rex cliff at Kelingking Beach, Angel's Billabong natural infinity pool, and Broken Beach with lunch included.

Day 3
Water Sports at Benoa & Uluwatu Cliff Temple

Morning water sports in Tanjung Benoa (Banana Boat & Parasailing). In the evening, head to the dramatic sea cliffs of Uluwatu Temple to watch the famous Kecak Fire Dance at sunset, followed by a Jimbaran Bay seafood dinner.

Day 4
Transfer to Ubud, Jungle Swing & Rice Terraces

Check into your luxury Ubud Private Pool Villa. Soar above the canopy on the iconic Bali Jungle Swing, walk the emerald Tegallalang Rice Terraces, and visit the sacred Tirta Empul water temple.

Day 5
Floating Villa Breakfast & Tegenungan Waterfall

Enjoy an Instagram-worthy floating breakfast delivered directly to your private villa pool. Explore the lush Tegenungan Waterfall, Ubud Art Market, and indulge in a rejuvenating 2-hour Balinese couple massage.

Day 6
Souvenir Shopping & Departure Transfer

Enjoy your morning coffee amidst tropical greenery. Private transfer to Bali Airport with sweet memories of the Island of the Gods.
